About Device & Controls

Renowned high-quality supplier & trader of electronic components and control solutions.

Device & Controls is one of the most trusted and renowned high-quality suppliers and traders of electronic components and industrial control solutions in India, headquartered in Kolkata, West Bengal.

Our Story

Established as a specialized industrial supply company, Device & Controls has grown into a comprehensive one-stop solution for all electronic component and automation needs. From our base in Kolkata at 160, Jodhpur Garden, we serve industries across West Bengal and beyond.

Our strength lies in our deep partnerships with the world's foremost automation and control brands — Siemens, Schneider Electric, Allen-Bradley, ABB, Omron, YASKAWA, and many more — enabling us to source and supply authentic, high-quality products at competitive prices.

Our Mission

To be the most reliable and responsive industrial electronics supplier in Eastern India, delivering quality products on time with complete transparency and industry expertise.

Our Vision

To become the foremost channel partner and supplier for industrial automation and control solutions across India, bridging global brands with local industrial requirements.

Core Values

  • We Value Time — Streamlined operations for on-time, every-time delivery
  • Always Deliver Quality Products — Sourcing only from top-tier global brands
  • Maintain Complete Transparency — Clear communication and honest updates
  • Vast Industry Experience — Deep expertise in industrial automation and control
  • Customer-First Approach — Every inquiry and order handled with utmost care
  • Authentic Products — 100% genuine merchandise from authorized distributors

Our Reach

Based in Kolkata, we serve customers across West Bengal, Eastern India and beyond. Our logistics network and established supplier relationships allow us to fulfill orders promptly, whether for a single component or a bulk industrial procurement.
